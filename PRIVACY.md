# Easy JS8 Privacy Policy

Easy JS8 is made by Carson VA3OSO. The developer collects no data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or crash reports.

## What Easy JS8 sends over the internet

With **Enable spotting to reporting networks (JS8NET, PSKReporter, etc)** on, Easy JS8 sends:

- to PSK Reporter (pskreporter.info): your callsign, your grid locator, your station info text, and the stations you hear.
- to JS8NET (spot.js8call.com): the same, plus the text of CQ calls and of messages to a group (@ALLCALL, @APRSIS and others) that your radio receives. The text of messages addressed to one callsign is not sent.

With **Enable spotting @APRSIS messages to the APRS-IS network** on, Easy JS8 sends decoded @APRSIS messages to APRS-IS (aprs2.net).

Both are on by default. Turn them off in **Station > Reporting > Network Services**. These services publish spots on public maps. Easy JS8 sends this data without encryption.

Your grid locator can be up to 10 characters long after a GPS update, which marks your position to within a few metres.

Copies installed from outside Google Play check GitHub for a newer version. That check sends no personal data.

## Permissions

- **Microphone**: receives audio from your radio.
- **Location**: GPS sets your grid locator.
- **Bluetooth and USB**: connect to your radio.
- **Notifications**: show that Easy JS8 is running, and show messages addressed to you.

## Deleting your data

The developer holds none of your data. Spots are kept by PSK Reporter, JS8NET and APRS-IS. Contact them to have yours removed.
